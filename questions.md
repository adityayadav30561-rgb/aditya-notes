## PIQ 2 – Father: Agriculture & Current Affairs
- What is MSP? :: Minimum Support Price – the minimum price at which the government buys a crop from farmers, to protect them from price crashes.
- Who recommends MSP? :: Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP).
- Who announces MSP? :: The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approves; Government of India announces.
- What is the difference between MSP and market price? :: MSP is a government-assured floor price; market price is set by supply and demand in mandis and can be higher or lower.
- What is procurement? :: Government buying of crops at MSP (through FCI and state agencies) for buffer stocks and the public distribution system.
- What is a mandi? :: A regulated agricultural market (APMC) where farmers sell produce through commission agents/traders.
- What is e-NAM? :: National Agriculture Market – online trading platform (2016) linking APMC mandis across India.
- What is PM-KISAN? :: PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (2019): ₹6,000 a year to eligible farmers in three instalments of ₹2,000 via DBT.
- What is crop insurance? :: Insurance that pays farmers for crop loss due to natural calamities, pests or disease.
- What is PMFBY? :: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (2016): low premium (2% Kharif, 1.5% Rabi, 5% commercial crops); rest paid by government.
- What are agricultural subsidies? :: Government support to cut farm costs: fertiliser, power, irrigation, seed, credit (interest subvention) and machinery subsidies.
- What is irrigation efficiency? :: Getting maximum crop output per unit of water by reducing losses (e.g. drip/sprinkler, lining canals).
- What is drip irrigation? :: Water delivered drop by drop to plant roots through pipes and emitters; saves 30–60% water and fertiliser.
- What is agricultural mechanisation? :: Using machines (tractors, harvesters, seed drills) instead of manual labour to raise productivity.
- How can technology improve farming? :: Drones for spraying, soil testing, weather/market apps, precision farming, better seeds, e-NAM, AI advisories.
- What are the major challenges facing Indian agriculture? :: Small landholdings, water stress, low productivity, price volatility, climate change, poor storage, debt, low mechanisation.

## General – Basic Technical
- What is the difference between 4G and 5G? :: 5G: much higher speed (up to Gbps), lower latency (about 1 ms), more connected devices; uses higher frequencies. 4G: up to about 100 Mbps, higher latency.
- Explain the Pythagoras theorem to an eight-year-old. :: In a right-angled triangle, the square on the longest side equals the two other squares added: 3×3 + 4×4 = 5×5.
- Where are integration and differentiation used in daily life? :: Differentiation: rate of change – speed, growth rates. Integration: total accumulated – distance from speed, area, volume of tanks.
- How does a fan regulator work? :: Controls the voltage/current to the fan motor; modern electronic regulators use a TRIAC and capacitors to cut power, old ones used resistors.
- What are the laws of thermodynamics, and where do you see them in everyday life? :: 0th: thermal equilibrium (thermometer). 1st: energy conserved (engine, cooking). 2nd: heat flows hot to cold, entropy increases (fridge needs power, tea cools). 3rd: absolute zero unreachable.
- What does the CC of an engine mean? :: Cubic capacity – total swept volume of the cylinders; more CC generally means more power.
