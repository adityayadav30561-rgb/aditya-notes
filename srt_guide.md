# SSB SRT – Master Sheet
> Understand exactly what is given, use what is available, act decisively, solve the problem, complete the responsibility, and don't invent problems that aren't there.

## 1. Aim & golden rules
- **Show a person who:** understands the exact situation · creates no extra problems · uses available resources · acts practically and in time · shows courage only when needed · is resourceful, not dependent on authorities · handles people well · completes the task · learns from mistakes · prevents repetition.
- **Let the SRT favour you.** Don't add danger, difficulty, hostility, injury, criminal intent or complications that aren't in the stimulus – but stay realistic.
- **Don't undersolve or oversolve.** Simple problem → simple answer. Real need for initiative → full action chain.

## 2. Thinking process (6 steps)
- **1 Read exact facts:** what happened, what is and isn't written, my responsibility, the constraint. Never fill gaps with assumptions.
- **2 Spot the OLQ tested:** resourcefulness, courage, presence of mind, initiative, responsibility, leadership, cooperation, social adaptability, communication, tact, determination, perseverance, decision-making, practical intelligence, self-confidence, sense of duty, time management.
- **3 Use available resources:** "What can I use right now?" (checklist in section 5).
- **4 Solve the constraint, don't restate it.** Cycle puncture: not "wait for mechanic" but load it on another cycle's carrier, sit holding it, redistribute luggage, get it repaired en route.
- **5 Complete the situation:** not just "called police" but "got culprit arrested" / "ensured hospital admission" / "completed exam" / "reached safely".
- **6 Return to the original task:** accident → office; emergency → revise → exam; intruder → revision → exam; sick mother → treatment → resume duty. Manage both duties; never abandon either (guest + exam, injured person + SSB).
- **Master formula:** FACT → CONSTRAINT → RESOURCE → ACTION → PEOPLE → AUTHORITY → OUTCOME → LEARNING (don't force every stage).
- **Simple SRT:** Action → Outcome. **Complex SRT:** Immediate action → resourcefulness → coordination → authority → completion → prevention. **Chain:** Observe → Act → Arrange → Inform → Complete → Follow up.

## 3. Writing rules
- **Tense = stimulus tense.** "You find… / You would…" → action verbs: Check, inform, arrange… Past narration ("He noticed…") → past tense is fine. Default style: verb, verb, verb.
- **No filler:** remained calm · maintained friendship/confidence · continued writing · took necessary action · secured the area · handled the situation · did the needful · wore life jacket (already worn if boating). Show the action: "Clarified conflicting orders with immediate senior, followed coordinated instruction."
- **Don't repeat the obvious:** "completed examination confidently", not "continued writing the exam".
- **Strong verbs:** arrange, assess, alert, assist, call, challenge, check, clarify, collect, coordinate, counsel, cover, detain, discuss, distribute, educate, encourage, ensure, explain, gather, guide, help, identify, inform, isolate, lead, locate, motivate, negotiate, observe, organise, overpower, persuade, protect, recover, repair, report, resolve, restrain, retrieve, secure, support, verify.
- **Thank** only those who really helped (friend's money → repay + thank; neighbours; authority that recovered item). Not mechanically.
- **Future caution** where it fits: avoid footboard · remain cautious · advise honest preparation · learn swimming · strengthen colony security.

## 4. Don't assume, don't create danger
- **Armed men stop you on a trek** – may be security: stop, show ID, permit and documents, explain purpose, continue trek.
- **Six Nagas with lathis running at you** – no invented attack or boar: raise hands, show ID and permit, explain visit, talk respectfully, continue.
- **"Car collided with a boy"** ≠ hit-and-run: check boy, basic first aid, continue work.
- **Brother lost in forest at night** – darkness is the only problem: shout his name, light flame torch, search nearby, locate, return together. No invented animals or injury.
- Don't invent train/bus speed, a new route when the same route must be used, or an unreadable number plate you "read".

## 5. Resourcefulness first; authorities only when needed
- **Ask first:** can I solve it myself? Don't default to police, ambulance, authorities, technology, online payment or "calling someone".
- **Handle yourself:** seat confusion · missing briefcase · minor collision · friend's mistake · friend copying · tuition + exam · footboard incident.
- **Authorities needed (use the right one):** serious crime · fire · serious accident · armed criminals · theft · major disorder · railway crime · kidnapping · serious safety threat.
- **Inform only who needs to know:** serious accident → ambulance, police, family · minor incident → nobody · exam emergency → SSB/exam authority · sick mother → boss + family support · unconscious brother → treat first, tell parents once stable.
- **Helplines (only if relevant):** 100 Police · 101 Fire · 102 Ambulance · 139 Railway · 1091 Women · 1098 Child.
- **Checklist – People:** friends, family, neighbours, passengers, elders, colleagues, public. **Equipment:** tool kit, torch, blanket, extinguisher, cycle carrier, vehicle, PA system. **Time:** early morning, breaks, night, en route. **Money:** friends, relatives, token/partial payment, repay later. **Transport:** public, passing vehicle, companion's. **Knowledge:** technical skill, first aid, military training, electrical.

## 6. Courage, not recklessness
- **Good:** protecting someone · collective action against criminals when odds favour it · helping the injured · taking responsibility · leading a team · facing hardship.
- **Bad:** jumping from a fast train · entering a burning building for valuables · fighting armed criminals alone without reason · needless physical risk.
- **Formula:** courage + presence of mind + practical judgment. **People first, property second.**

## 7. Lost vs missing · GRP vs RPF · FIR
- **LOST → REPORT:** report to right authority → describe item → complaint → follow up/recover → remain cautious. Train → RPF · station → GRP · airport → airport authority/CISF.
- **MISSING → SEARCH:** search → enquire → retrace → locate → continue (ask passengers, search coach, toilet, luggage area, find briefcase, continue cautiously).
- **GRP** (Government Railway Police): crime, law and order on railway premises – theft at station. **RPF** (Railway Protection Force): security of railway property and passengers – incident on train.
- **FIR:** report where the offence happened (theft in Delhi → Delhi). Zero FIR = filed at any station, transferred to the right one. Don't write "FIR" in every theft SRT.

## 8. Response structures
- **Emergency:** assess → immediate action → assistance → authority → outcome. **Medical:** check → first aid (only if needed) → medical care → family → recovery.
- **Conflict:** bring together → listen → discuss → solution → harmony. **Family dispute:** discuss → elders → listen → solution → family harmony.
- **Financial:** immediate arrangement → alternative resource → fulfil obligation → repay.
- **Lost:** report → describe → complaint → recover → caution. **Missing:** search → enquire → locate → continue.
- **Exam:** use existing preparation → manage time → appear → succeed. **Team:** gather → analyse → plan → motivate → execute → win.
- **Crime:** observe → act → collective support → police → arrest/recovery.

## 9. Situation patterns
| People, family & conflict |
| Dispute (land, family) | Arrange meeting, involve elders, listen to both sides, present fair solution, resolve amicably. Never fight, take sides, threaten or escalate.
| Counselling (behaviour problem, e.g. kids miss school) | Counsel individually, understand reason, discuss with teachers, address difficulty, ensure regular attendance.
| Convincing (someone opposes a proposal) | Discuss qualities, arrange meeting, address concerns, persuade, gain consent.
| Parents chose another partner | Respect + persuasion + independence: discuss qualities, explain compatibility, arrange meeting, address concerns, gain consent, marry with family support. Never "disobey parents".
| Parents want early marriage, you want job | Explain career goals, seek support, secure job, marry with family consent.
| Quarrel with friend | Discuss issue, listen to his view, clarify misunderstanding, resolve amicably, continue normally (not "resume friendship").
| Friend's advice (e.g. be tactful with boss) | Consider feedback, assess independently, communicate confidently with boss, keep cordial relations. Don't follow blindly.
| Your mistake pointed out | Accept mistake, thank friend, correct it, avoid repetition.
| Friend copying in exam | Signal him to stop, complete exam, discuss consequences later, advise honest preparation.
| Riot / religious conflict | Pacify situation, gather representatives, listen to both sides, involve elders, resolve, promote harmony. No sides by majority/minority.
| GD – nobody listens | Listen, present points briefly with facts, ask their opinions, clarify differences, reach common conclusion.
| Friends arguing | Calm them, hear both views, clarify facts, give logical solution, resolve amicably.
| Political guest opposed | Don't force your choice: discuss concerns, explain suitability, seek consensus, invite mutually accepted guest.
| Friend pushes your vote | Listen, explain choice respectfully, keep independent decision, encourage thoughtful voting.
| Conflicting orders | Approach immediate senior, clarify both orders, follow final coordinated instruction, complete task.
| Accidents, fire & animals |
| Minor collision | Check boy, basic first aid, continue work. No ambulance/police unless injury shown.
| Ghastly accident | Check injured, first aid, pacify parties, call 102 and 100, inform families, ensure hospital admission, continue home.
| Hit-and-run (stated) | Check child, first aid, note colour/model/direction/features, call 102 and 100, inform parents, ensure admission, help police trace driver.
| Many people hurt | Check, first aid, pacify, move people from danger, medical help, police if serious, inform families, finish task, resume own work.
| Electrical fire (you're an electrician) | Switch off mains, isolate faulty wiring, CO₂ extinguisher, call 101, assist firefighters, inspect wiring, restore supply safely. Never water.
| Fire in house | Gather neighbours, call 101, switch off MCB, use extinguisher, assist firefighters, secure house. Never go in for valuables.
| Cobra in bathroom | Cover with towel, isolate bathroom, call snake catcher, get it removed, use after inspection.
| Cobra near brother's bed | Throw blanket over it, move brother away, close room, call snake catcher, ensure his safety.
| Step on cobra on trek | Step back, alert team, let it move away, continue cautiously. Never kill animals needlessly.
| Flame / torch | Gives light, may deter some wildlife – not guaranteed protection.
| Travel, train & water |
| Passenger claims your seat | Check both tickets, verify seat, find and guide him to his seat, settle amicably. No TTE needed.
| Thief jumps off train (speed not given) | Ask co-passengers to pull chain, get off, chase, overpower, recover purse, hand him to RPF.
| Chain pulling | Only if stopping truly helps (e.g. friend falls off moving train). Injury inside train: first aid, call 139, alert TTE, medical aid at next station, inform family, continue, advise caution.
| Friend falls from footboard (bus not moving) | Give him my hand, help him on, avoid footboard myself thereafter, continue journey.
| Fall into sea, can't swim | Stay afloat with life jacket, dog-paddle to boat, hold side, climb in, continue, learn swimming later.
| Cash via dangerous route (same route) | Simple clothes, conceal cash, split among companions, travel in morning by public transport, stay vigilant, reach safely.
| Crime & women's safety |
| Dacoits – you alone vs many | Survival first: hand over belongings, observe details, inform police, help recover. No heroic fight against odds.
| 2 gunmen vs 72 passengers | Unite passengers, distract gunmen, overpower collectively, secure weapons, inform RPF, hand over.
| Kidnapped, asked to sign ransom note | Don't refuse or provoke captors: sign, observe route and surroundings, escape at safe opportunity, call 100, help police trace dacoits.
| Robber on deserted road | One unarmed: distract, control, restrain, call 100, hand over. Armed: don't assume you can snatch the weapon. Many: give belongings, observe features, inform police, help identify/recover.
| With sister / girlfriend | Protect her first, use tactical action, get her safe, then deal with threat. Never sacrifice her: negotiate, use valuables as distraction, SOS, escape, get collective help. Both: send both to safety while handling threat.
| Friend harassed in bus | Handle threat first, then support friend: gather passengers, restrain goondas, protect friend, call 100, hand them over, pacify friend.
| Boys passing comments | Warn firmly, gather nearby people, confront behaviour, call 100, get them cautioned, reach home safely. Don't make her run.
| Intruders, exam next day | Alert neighbours, overpower intruders with them, call 100, get them arrested, continue revision, appear confidently, strengthen colony security.
| Exams, SSB, work & money |
| Ill before exam (already prepared) | Follow treatment, recover, revise prepared topics, appear confidently, pass.
| Guest arrives, exam tomorrow | Welcome, serve refreshments, arrange stay, study till syllabus done, attend him in breaks till family comes, appear, pass.
| Curfew on exam day | Show admit card to police, explain urgency, get passage, reach centre, appear, return safely. No need to call exam centre.
| Tuition + own exam | Don't cancel (students' exams are near too): teach as scheduled, revise in gaps, early morning and late night, finish syllabus, appear confidently.
| Injured person on way to SSB | First aid, stop passing vehicle, admit at hospital en route, inform SSB centre, reach on time.
| Certificates missing at SSB | Search belongings, vehicle and nearby places, enquire at reception, find them, attend interview.
| Certificates stolen at station | Report to GRP, give suitcase details, get FIR copy, inform SSB centre, use duplicate/digital copies, attend SSB, retrieve via GRP, stay cautious.
| Father can't fund studies | Seek scholarship, talk to teachers, part-time work, continue studies, get job, support father.
| Study loan, can't repay | Secure job, plan repayment, repay gradually, manage expenses, support family.
| Relative refuses loan for sister's marriage | Ask friends, arrange interest-free funds, complete marriage, repay and thank them.
| Son's hostel fee vs relative's urgent loan | Lend to relative, pay hostel token money, secure admission, pay balance later.
| Father dies in your final year | Shift mother to safe relative, involve elders, resolve dispute, complete final year, get job, support mother.
| Child facing hardship | Approach trusted adults/teachers, inform authorities, rescue mother, secure father's pension, continue school, part-time work only if age fits.
| Mother ill, boss denies leave | Don't fight boss: relative takes her to hospital, explain to boss, overtime/WFH, ensure treatment, resume duty.
| Brother unconscious, parents away | Don't panic parents first: call doctor, get nearby relative, take him to hospital, ensure recovery, inform parents on return, thank neighbours.
| Team losing (basketball) | Use timeout, analysis, strategy, practice, substitution, motivation: timeout, refreshments, revive energy, analyse opponents, change tactics, coordinate, lead from front, win.
| Captain injured (you're vice-captain) | Bring substitute, practise, assess opponents, plan, motivate, lead team, win.
| Organise picnic | Select site, fix date and budget, arrange transport, food and permissions, brief all, conduct smoothly, return on time.
| Military, NCC & village |
| Friend kept 20 rounds from range | Make him realise mistake, warn of consequences, secure rounds, verify count, ask about more, hand to armoury, record.
| Rifle disc near unit lines | Pick it up, inform superior, check area for more, deposit with armoury, record recovery.
| Helicopter crash near unit | Alert unit, reach site, assess casualties, first aid, inform medical team and authorities, evacuate injured, secure site.
| Enemy shelling (as officer) | Alert troops, order positions, inform senior, secure HVT, keep communication, assess, coordinate strong retaliation.
| Dacoits near NCC camp | Alert guard post, inform camp commander, warn personnel, secure arms and ammunition, take position, overpower dacoits.
| Person crawling into compound | Challenge, ask for ID, alert guard post, inform commander, detain for verification.
| Sentry duty | Stay alert, identify, communicate, protect assets, respond appropriately. Never abandon post.
| Locust attack | Gather farmers, cover crops with nets, approved insecticides, noise and smoke, protect crops, assess losses, restore fields.
| Village epidemic | Start sanitation drive, educate on hygiene via PA system, assist medical check-ups, control epidemic.

## 10. Final check before writing
- **Facts:** only what is written? · **Tense:** matches stimulus? · **Resourceful:** solved it myself? · **OLQs:** shown through action, not adjectives? · **Realism:** would a real person do it? · **Completion:** situation closed? · **Original task:** returned to it? · **No overcomplication:** invented nothing? · **No filler:** every action adds value? · **Outcome:** sensible result?
- **Remember:** use communication for conflicts, counselling for behaviour problems · consider advice, don't follow blindly · courageous when required, never reckless · don't make every problem a police problem · don't underplay a truly serious one · learn and prevent repetition.
