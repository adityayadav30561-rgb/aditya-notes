## Core facts
- **What**: premier forum for **international economic cooperation**; advanced + emerging economies.
- **Members**: **19 countries + EU + AU = 21** (name kept "G20"; AU joined **2023** as 21st). Never say "20 countries".
- **Weight**: **~85% world GDP · 75%+ trade · ~2/3 population**.
- **Nature**: **informal** – no treaty, secretariat, HQ or staff; works by consensus; outcomes **non-binding**; presidency rotates yearly. Not UN-like, **not a military alliance** (no NATO Art. 5).
- **Origin**: Asian crisis 1997–98 → **1999** (Finance Ministers + Central Bank Governors) → 2008 global financial crisis → **Leaders' level**; **1st summit Washington 2008**; **Pittsburgh 2009** named it "premier forum".
- **Agenda grew**: finance → trade, climate, energy, health, agriculture, digital, tax, infrastructure, jobs, food security, financial inclusion.
- **Member list**: Americas – Canada, Mexico, USA, Argentina, Brazil · Europe – France, Germany, Italy, UK, Russia, Türkiye · Asia – China, India, Indonesia, Japan, S. Korea, Saudi Arabia · Others – Australia, South Africa · **+ EU, AU (55 nations)**.

## How it works
- **Presidency**: sets priorities, hosts meetings + Leaders' Summit. **Troika** = past + current + next → continuity (2025: Brazil–S. Africa–USA).
- **Finance Track** (FMs + Central Bank Governors) = **money**: macro policy, financial stability, tax, debt, infra/development finance, IMF/WB.
- **Sherpa Track** (Sherpa = leader's senior envoy) = **everything else**: health, education, jobs, climate, energy, agri, digital, tourism, anti-corruption, women.
- **Year cycle**: working groups → ministerials → engagement groups → negotiations → **Summit → Leaders' Declaration** (political commitment, not treaty).
- **Engagement groups**: B20 business · T20 think tanks · Y20 youth · W20 women · C20 civil society · L20 labour · S20 science.

## India's presidency 2023 ★
- **1 Dec 2022 – 30 Nov 2023**; summit **New Delhi, 9–10 Sep 2023**.
- **Theme**: **"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam – One Earth, One Family, One Future"** (Maha Upanishad) → Earth = environment · Family = humanity · Future = shared prosperity.
- **African Union → permanent member**: Africa = population, resources, critical minerals, food/energy role, development needs → permanent collective voice for Global South.
- **Global South** focus; hosted **Voice of Global South Summit 2023**.
- **DPI**: Aadhaar (ID) + UPI (payments) + DigiLocker (documents); **JAM (Jan Dhan–Aadhaar–Mobile) + UPI** = financial inclusion; G20 endorsed DPI/financial-inclusion recommendations.
- **MDB reform** (World Bank etc.) · **debt** – Global Sovereign Debt Roundtable (G20 + IMF + WB) · food & energy security · SDGs · climate finance.
- **LiFE** (Lifestyle for Environment): individual action – less waste, save water/energy, sustainable consumption & mobility.
- **IMEC** (India–Middle East–Europe Economic Corridor): India, US, Saudi, UAE, EU partners; shipping, rail, digital, energy links. Frame as "**diversified connectivity & supply chains**", not merely anti-BRI.
- **Global Biofuel Alliance**: biofuel markets, tech sharing, energy transition.
- **New Delhi Declaration**: strong, sustainable, balanced, inclusive growth; SDG 2030 push; climate, food, health (medical countermeasures, production in developing nations), digital, debt, MDBs; sovereignty, territorial integrity, peaceful dispute settlement.

## Key issues (one line each)
- **Climate**: developing → historical emitters must give finance + tech; developed → all big emitters must cut. **India = climate action + development + climate justice**.
- **Global South**: developing Asia/Africa/LatAm – poverty, debt, climate finance, tech access, representation. Reform IMF/WB/UNSC to reflect today, not post-WWII order.
- **Debt**: high debt & rates, weak currencies, food/energy shocks → Common Framework, restructuring, MDB & concessional finance.
- **Russia–Ukraine**: economic forum with divided members; war hits energy, food, fertiliser, inflation, so it enters agenda. Stand: international law, UN Charter, sovereignty.
- **China**: can't coordinate without it (economy, trade, manufacturing, P5); concerns – supply-chain concentration, trade imbalance, tech rivalry, minerals. India engages but guards own interest.
- **Terrorism**: not core; terror financing, money laundering, transparency – "terror has an economic dimension".
- **Health**: post-COVID preparedness, financing, vaccines. **Women**: labour participation, entrepreneurship, digital/financial inclusion – women-led development.
- **Food & supply chains**: grain, fertiliser, fuel, shipping; chips, pharma, minerals, electronics → Make in India / Atmanirbhar Bharat.
- **Critical minerals**: lithium, cobalt, nickel, rare earths → batteries, EVs, renewables, defence. **AI**: safety, governance, data, IP, jobs, standards, digital divide.

## Comparisons
- **vs G7**: G7 = 7 advanced Western democracies; G20 adds emerging (India, China, Brazil, Indonesia, SA) → broader. Different purposes, not replacements.
- **vs BRICS**: emerging/Global South group, no US/EU. India in both → **multi-alignment**.
- **vs UN**: 193 states, universal; peace, security, law, rights. Don't rank – mandates differ.
- **vs NATO**: collective defence vs economic cooperation.
- **vs IMF/WB**: IMF = monetary stability, WB = development finance; G20 = political coordination (works with IMF, WB, WTO, WHO, OECD, FSB).

## India angle
- **Why it matters**: trade, investment, energy, tech (AI, DPI), supply chains, food/fertiliser, voice in global decisions, Global South leadership, Viksit Bharat needs (investment, tech, infra, stable world economy).
- **Strategic autonomy**: one forum to engage US, Russia, EU, Japan, Gulf, ASEAN; multi-layered diplomacy (UN, G20, BRICS, SCO, Quad, ASEAN, bilateral).
- **Defence link** (G20 itself ≠ defence): minerals → chips → defence manufacturing · energy security → economic → national security · resilient supply chains → defence preparedness.
- **Chain**: G20 → world economy → India's growth → trade/energy/supply chains → tech & minerals → national security → strategic autonomy.

## Strengths vs limitations
- **Strengths**: economic weight · developed + emerging mix · crisis coordination (2008) · flexible, less bureaucratic · Global South voice (AU) · policy coordination (tax, debt, climate finance, digital).
- **Limitations**: no secretariat · consensus hard (US–China, Russia–West, North–South) · non-binding · many nations outside · geopolitical rivalry · implementation gap.

## IO questions → answer lines
- **Tell me about G20 (60 s)**: definition → 1999/2008 → 19 + EU + AU, 85% GDP/75% trade → agenda → India 2023 theme, AU, DPI, Global South → 2026 US presidency, Miami, Dec.
- **21 members but "G20"?** Was 19 + EU; AU added 2023, name retained.
- **Why not condemn Russia?** Economic forum, divergent members; seeks consensus on economy while upholding international law & UN Charter.
- **Why can't it stop wars?** Not its mandate – but wars' economic effects (energy, food, inflation) reach its agenda.
- **Biggest problems?** (1) Geopolitical fragmentation (2) North–South divide on debt & climate finance (3) Implementation gap.
- **What should it focus on?** Economic stability, resilient supply chains, debt sustainability, energy/food security, climate finance, responsible emerging tech.
- **India's achievements?** AU entry · Global South voice · DPI & financial inclusion · MDB reform & debt. Don't rank; AU = key institutional gain.
- **India's role?** Major emerging economy · North–South bridge · Global South voice · DPI exporter · development-compatible climate action · reform advocate.
- **Dominated by the powerful?** Weight follows economic size, but emerging economies + AU are in; outsiders' concerns are valid → can't replace the UN.
- **Does it have power?** Big influence, no enforcement – depends on consensus & national implementation.
- **What did you learn?** IR = finding common ground among differing interests; economy, security, tech and climate are interlinked.
